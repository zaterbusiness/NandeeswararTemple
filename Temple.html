A Sacred Abode of Lord Shiva

Sri Nandeeswarar Temple is an ancient Hindu temple located in Adambakkam, Chennai. Dedicated to Lord Shiva as Sri Nandeeswarar and Goddess Avudai Nayagi, the temple is associated with the ancient Chola period and the legend of Sage Bhringi.

History & Legend

The Chola Connection

The temple is connected with the Chola dynasty and is approximately 950 years old.

Inscriptions

Historical inscriptions associated with Rajaraja Chola I are linked to this temple.

Sage Bhringi Maharishi

The legend of Sage Bhringi Maharishi is central to the sthala puranam of the temple.

Lord Shiva as Nandi

Lord Shiva is said to have appeared before Sage Bhringi in the form of Nandi.

Bringi Malai & Parangi Malai

The tradition draws a historical connection between Bringi Malai and Parangi Malai.
